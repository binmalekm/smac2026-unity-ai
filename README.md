## App Idea
Unity AI is a family-support mobile application designed for the SMAC 2026 competition.

The app includes features such as:
- Family profiles
- Medication reminders
- Daily routines
- Family mood tracking
- Emotional support
- AI family assistant
- Optional family location features

## Purpose
The goal of Unity AI is to use artificial intelligence and smart mobile technology to strengthen family bonds, improve communication, and support family members in their daily routines.
